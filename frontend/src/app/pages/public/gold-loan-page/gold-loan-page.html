Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about loan rates, vault storage, repayment waterfall, and collateral security.

How is the interest calculated?

Interest is calculated strictly on simple interest terms, starting from 1% per month (12% per annum) on the exact outstanding principal balance. We do not charge compounding interest or hidden administration charges.

How secure is my pledged gold in your vault?

Your ornaments are sealed in high-security, tamper-evident barcoded bags in your presence. They are placed in bank-grade, fireproof, drill-resistant safes under 24/7 dual CCTV surveillance with 100% comprehensive insurance coverage.

Can I close my loan earlier than the maturity date?

Yes, absolutely. You can prepay or close your loan at any point during the loan tenure with zero foreclosure fees or prepayment penalties. You only pay interest for the exact duration the funds were utilized.

What documents do I need to bring for instant cash disbursal?

All you need is your original gold jewellery and any valid government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, or Passport). For loans exceeding ₹50,000, your PAN Card is also required.

Can I make partial repayments towards my principal?

Yes! Any partial principal repayment immediately reduces your outstanding loan balance, which in turn reduces your monthly interest obligation right from the day of payment.
